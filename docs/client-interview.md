# Client Interview

## Interview Details
- **Date:** 2026-05-27
- **Client:** Verónica Mora Lezcano
- **Method:** Email Word Document
- **Interviewer:** Bryan Morales Cascante

## Purpose
The purpose of this document is to gather key information from the client in order to define the functional and non-functional requirements of the CampusFest website. The responses obtained will serve as the foundation for the design, development, and implementation of the platform, ensuring that the final product meets the client's needs and expectations.
